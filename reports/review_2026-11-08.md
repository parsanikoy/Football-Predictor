## Rules (pre-declared)

- The alternatives list is fixed in `src/fpredict/review.py`. Adding one is a deliberate decision, not a reaction to one week.
- Data is split by kick-off date: the newest 40% is the test set.
- An alternative passes a review only if it is better on the calibration set, better on the test set with the corrected range entirely above zero, better on test log loss, and there is enough data.
- It is suggested only after passing at two consecutive reviews.
- The test set grows every week and is reused, so a pass is strong evidence, not proof. After a change is adopted, judge it on matches played after the change.
