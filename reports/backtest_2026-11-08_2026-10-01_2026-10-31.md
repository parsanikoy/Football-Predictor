## Limitations

- Team and league statistics are PitchAPI's current values, which may include revisions made after the match (§28). Results, not stats, decide outcomes, so this cannot change who won a bet.
- Only the current season's league matches are available, so early-season fixtures where a team had played fewer than five league games cannot be modelled.
- User-supplied league averages are not applied here, because they describe the league today, not before each match. User-supplied match stats are applied.
- These are fixed Version 1 coefficients that were not fitted to these matches, so this is an out-of-sample check of Version 1. Re-using these same matches to tune Version 2 would make them in-sample.
